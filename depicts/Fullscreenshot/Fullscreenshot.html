📸 Fullscreenshot

The ultimate native scrolling screenshot experience for iOS.

✨ Description

Have you ever wanted to capture an entire conversation, a long list of tweaks, or an endless article, but hated manually stitching multiple images together? Fullscreenshot solves this natively.

Fullscreenshot deeply integrates into iOS so that when you trigger a standard screenshot (Volume Up + Power), it automatically scans the screen for a scrollable area and smoothly captures the entire length of the page—saving one massive, high-quality image directly to your Camera Roll.

🚀 Core Features
  • Seamless Native Integration: No extra apps or weird gestures required. Just take a screenshot like you normally do (Volume Up + Power).
  • Smart Stitching Engine: Bypasses "Estimated Row Heights" bugs by silently pre-measuring the entire scroll view before capturing, ensuring no cutoffs and no repeating sticky headers.
  • Native Screenshot Blocker: Intelligent daemon hook that completely suppresses default Apple screenshots, keeping your Photos app uncluttered by duplicate short images (Supports iOS 16 ScreenshotServicesService).
  • Memory Safe: Automatically scales down image resolution on massive captures (over 12,000 pixels) to prevent Out-Of-Memory (OOM) crashes.
  • Haptic Feedback: Satisfying Apple-style Heavy Impact and Success haptic vibrations when a capture starts and finishes.
  • Custom Toast Notifications: Clean, non-intrusive popup confirming your massive screenshot was saved successfully.
⚙️ Preferences

Lightweight preferences panel built into Settings:

  • Enable/Disable Tweak: Master killswitch to toggle the tweak.
  • Save Native Screenshot: Option to keep original short iOS screenshot or discard it automatically.
  • Haptic Feedback: Toggle haptic vibration feedback on or off.
  • Max Screenshot Height: Adjustable slider (up to 30,000px) limiting screenshot height before scrolling stops.
